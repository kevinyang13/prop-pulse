Common questions
Is my financial data secure? ▾
Your tax profile is encrypted at rest in Supabase and never shared with third parties. We only use it to calculate your analysis on demand — we don't sell or profile your data.
Can I cancel anytime? ▾
Yes. Cancel from Settings → Subscription at any time. You'll retain Pro access until the end of your billing period.
How accurate is the tax math? ▾
PropPulse uses 2025 IRS brackets, standard depreciation rules (27.5-year straight-line), and the PAL phase-out schedule. Results are estimates — always consult a CPA before making investment decisions.
What happens to my free analyses if I upgrade? ▾
They stay saved in your account. Upgrading adds unlimited new analyses on top of what you've already done.
Do you offer a refund? ▾
Yes — within 7 days of your first charge. Email support@proppulse.io and we'll process it within 24 hours.
